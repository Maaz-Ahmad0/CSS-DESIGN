The Alchemist

Book Name : The Alchemist
Author Name : Paulo Coelho
Published Year : 1988
Genre : Fiction / Adventure
About the Book

The Alchemist is a beautiful story about a young shepherd who travels across the world in search of a treasure. It is not just about finding a treasure, but also about discovering your dreams, your purpose and the true meaning of life.

Why I Like This Book

I like this book because it teaches us to follow our dreams and never give up. The simple language and deep messages make it a special read. It also motivates me to be a better version of myself.
